Where the data comes from
Observations
GOES-19 ABI and Himawari-9 AHI L1b radiances (NOAA, and JMA for Himawari), read through NODD on AWS Open Data, which is open to the public.
JMA's own P-Tree channel carries a research-and-education-only condition; it is not used here.
Ancillary fields
Skin temperature: GFS 0.25° TMP:surface (NOAA, via NODD).
Emissivity: CAMEL V003, Borbas et al. 2023, NASA LP DAAC, under EOSDIS data use guidance.
Algorithms
DEBRA: Miller et al. 2017.
Dust RGB: Lensky & Rosenfeld 2008, in the EUMETSAT band combination.
Basemap
Natural Earth, public domain, drawn with cartopy. Boundaries are cartographic, not a statement of any position on them.
Software
shachen and shachen-operational, both Apache-2.0.

What is published here is modified data — a derived product of this pipeline, not original or unaltered NOAA, JMA or NASA data, and no endorsement or affiliation is implied. Reuse these composites and fields freely with credit; the observations themselves stay under their own providers' terms.
