DEBRA Dust RGB

a published scan loaded here on screen

Dust is coloured by detection confidence over an unmodified background; the Dust RGB is the same scene under the classic recipe, for comparison. Every scan here was produced unattended, from open archives, and nothing on this page is an official product of the agencies below.

Where this comes from

Observations
GOES-19 ABI L1b radiances, NOAA — NODD on AWS Open Data. A work of the US government, in the public domain.
Himawari-9 AHI L1b radiances, Japan Meteorological Agency, redistributed by NOAA on AWS Open Data under JMA's terms for Himawari data.
Ancillary fields
Skin temperature: GFS 0.25° TMP:surface, NOAA — NODD on AWS Open Data.
Emissivity: CAMEL CAM5K30EM V003, a monthly climatology from NASA LP DAAC, courtesy of NASA Earthdata.
Algorithms
DEBRA, the Dynamic Enhancement with Background Reduction Algorithm — Miller, S. D., Bankert, R. L., Solbrig, J. E., Forsythe, J. M., Noh, Y.-J., and Grasso, L. (2017), J. Geophys. Res. Atmos. 122, doi:10.1002/2017JD027365.
Dust RGB — Lensky, I. M., and Rosenfeld, D. (2008), Atmos. Chem. Phys. 8, 6739–6753, doi:10.5194/acp-8-6739-2008, in the EUMETSAT band combination.
Basemap
Coastlines and boundaries from Natural Earth, public domain, drawn with cartopy. Boundaries carry no political endorsement.
Software
shachen, an open implementation of DEBRA, run by shachen-operational. Both Apache-2.0.

The composites and fields published here are derived products of this pipeline, not the source data. Reuse them freely with credit to the providers above; the observations themselves remain under their providers' own terms.
